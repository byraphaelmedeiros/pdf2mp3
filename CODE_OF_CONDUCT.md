# Code of Conduct

This project does **not** adopt a formal Code of Conduct.

We believe in personal responsibility, freedom of expression, and voluntary collaboration.
Contributors are expected to interact respectfully and handle disagreements as independent adults.
Discuss ideas and technical decisions on their merits, and allow room for different opinions.

There are no enforced community rules beyond the applicable law.
Participation is voluntary: everyone is free to disagree, fork the project under
its license, or step away. Contributions are welcome; accepting a contribution
remains a technical and maintenance decision for the maintainer.

For questions or direct, private contact: pdf2mp3@byraphaelmedeiros.com.
